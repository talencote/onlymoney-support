Privacy Policy for OnlyMoney

Effective date: August 9, 2026

OnlyMoney is a personal-finance record-keeping application provided by Maks Vitko (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains how OnlyMoney handles information.

1. Data stored by OnlyMoney

OnlyMoney lets you enter financial information such as account names, balances, currencies, categories, transaction amounts, dates, and notes. This information is stored locally in the application’s private storage on your device.

OnlyMoney does not require a user account and does not connect to your bank. We do not operate a server that automatically receives your financial records.

2. Data collection and tracking

The current version of OnlyMoney does not collect personal data, financial data, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking information from the application. The application does not contain third-party advertising or analytics services.

3. Manual backups

You can manually export a backup and choose where to save or share it using Apple’s system interfaces. Backup files may contain account names, balances, categories, transaction history, and notes.

The current OnlyMoney backup format is not encrypted. Anyone who obtains a backup file may be able to read its contents. You are responsible for selecting a secure storage location, controlling access to the file, and deleting copies you no longer need.

OnlyMoney does not automatically upload backup files to us. A storage provider or sharing service selected by you may process the file under its own privacy policy.

4. Support communications

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address, message, and any information you choose to include. This information is used only to respond to your request and maintain necessary support records. Do not send financial records or an OnlyMoney backup unless you understand that it may contain sensitive financial information and we have specifically asked for it as part of resolving your request.

5. Data retention and deletion

Financial records remain on your device until you delete them in the application, replace them through a restore, erase application data, or uninstall the application, subject to the behavior of your device backups and storage services.

Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support history, prevent abuse, or satisfy legal obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us, subject to applicable legal requirements.

6. Children

OnlyMoney is a general personal-finance utility and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

7. Security

OnlyMoney relies on Apple platform protections for its local application storage. No method of storage is guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your device protected and store exported backup files securely.

8. International use

Because the application does not automatically transmit your financial records to us, those records remain under your control on your device and in any backup locations you select. Email support and third-party storage services you choose may process information in other countries under their own terms.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when OnlyMoney’s functionality or legal obligations change. The updated policy will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Material changes to data handling will also be reflected in the App Store privacy information and, when appropriate, in the application.
