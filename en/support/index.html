OnlyMoney Support

OnlyMoney is an offline-first personal-finance tracker for iPhone and iPad.

Contact

For help, bug reports, or feedback, email tlc.studio.dev@gmail.com (opens email app).

Please include:

  • your OnlyMoney version;
  • your iOS or iPadOS version;
  • your device model;
  • the steps that led to the problem;
  • a screenshot, if it does not reveal sensitive information.

Protect your information. Do not email account balances, transaction history, or an OnlyMoney backup unless support specifically asks for it and you understand that the file may contain sensitive, unencrypted financial information.

Frequently asked questions

Does OnlyMoney connect to my bank?

No. Transactions are entered manually, and no bank account connection is required.

Does OnlyMoney require internet access or registration?

No. The current app works locally and does not require a user account.

Where is my data stored?

Your financial records are stored in the app’s private local storage on your device.

How do I create a backup?

Open Accounts, open Settings, select Backup and Restore, then select Create Backup. Choose a secure destination using the system file interface.

Are backup files encrypted?

No. The current backup format is not encrypted. Anyone with access to the file may be able to read its financial contents. Store it securely.

What happens when I restore a backup?

Restore fully replaces the current OnlyMoney accounts, categories, and transactions with the contents of the selected backup. Review the summary and warning carefully before confirming.

Can support recover deleted data?

No. OnlyMoney does not keep a server copy of your financial records. Recovery is possible only from backups available to you or through device-level backup mechanisms you control.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual records in the app where available, restore another dataset, or delete the application and its data through the device. Files previously exported by you must be deleted separately from their storage locations.
