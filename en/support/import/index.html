Move from Another App

Use an AI service of your choice to turn another app’s export into an OnlyMoney backup, following our guide.

You can also add history to your existing OnlyMoney data: attach a current .onlymoneybackup together with the other app’s export. The AI combines them into one new backup before restoration.

Download AI instructions (.md, English)

Restoring replaces all current OnlyMoney accounts, categories, and transactions. Save a backup of your current data first. AI can make mistakes: check balances, dates, transfers, and any estimated currency conversions. A valid file does not guarantee accurate history.

Before you start

Export the most complete history available, including accounts, currencies, categories, transfers, and opening or dated closing balances. CSV, XLSX, or a documented JSON export usually gives better results than screenshots or PDF. Keep the original files unchanged. Never share passwords or bank credentials.

Step by step

  1. Download the Markdown instructions using the button above. The file is in English, but ask the AI to explain everything in your language.
  2. Choose an AI service that can read the whole export, execute code, and return downloadable files. ChatGPT is one example; no particular provider, paid plan, or free-tier availability is required or guaranteed.
  3. Attach the instructions and export files. To keep your current OnlyMoney history, attach its current backup too. Ask for an analysis first.
  4. Review the proposed accounts, categories, base currency and any approximate conversions. Tell the AI which exported accounts match existing accounts, then approve the plan.
  5. After approval, the AI creates one valid .onlymoneybackup containing the supplied existing history plus added records, and a short summary. Duplicate searching and exact balance reconciliation are not required.
  6. In OnlyMoney, create a separate backup of current data. Then open Settings → Backup and Restore → Restore from Backup. Review the summary and replacement warning before confirming.
  7. Open the restored accounts and history to check the result. Keep the original export and previous backup. Overlapping histories are added as supplied, so duplicates and approximate balances are possible.

Message to send with your files

Follow the attached OnlyMoney guide. Analyze the export and any attached current OnlyMoney backup, explain the structure and proposed mapping, and ask about account matching and base currency where needed. Propose practical defaults and approximate conversions; do not investigate duplicate history or require exact balances. Wait for my approval, then create one valid combined backup. Explain in my language.

Missing data and currency conversions

If historical currency equivalents are missing, the AI proposes a simple approximate conversion in the plan. For matched accounts it keeps the existing opening balance; for new accounts it uses the exported opening balance or zero. Missing balances and differences from source totals do not block generation. Debts, investments and unsupported records may need a simple explained representation or exclusion.

Check the report

The summary lists retained and added records, account mappings, the chosen base currency, approximate conversions and exclusions. The goal is a usable combined database; no duplicate investigation or penny-perfect reconciliation is performed. The file must still satisfy OnlyMoney’s structural validation.

Your Data and External AI

OnlyMoney does not send your export to us or to an AI service. If you upload it yourself, that service processes it under its own privacy terms and settings. Review them before sharing financial data. Exports and backups may contain unencrypted financial information.

Review the provider’s retention, training, deletion, and regional processing settings before uploading. Deleting data in OnlyMoney does not delete files or conversations held by that service. This website has no financial-file upload form; opening it uses your browser and GitHub Pages hosting.
